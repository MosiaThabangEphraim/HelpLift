HELPLIFT - SLIDE-READY SUMMARY

SLIDE 1: WHAT IS HELPLIFT?

HelpLift connects verified community organizations with people who want to help.

- Organizations post community needs and updates.
- Individuals and businesses can donate money, pledge goods or services, or contact organizations.
- Administrators review and moderate activity to support transparency and trust.
- Tagline: "Giving made transparent. Impact made real."


SLIDE 2: TECHNOLOGIES USED

- Frontend: Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript
- UI and styling: Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Radix UI
- Backend: Next.js API routes for application logic, validation, payments, email and integrations
- Database and platform services: Supabase with PostgreSQL, authentication, file storage and Row Level Security (RLS)
- AI: Google Gemini for the Lifty assistant, AI need writer, photo-to-pledge assistance and on-demand translation
- Payments: PayFast, PayPal and EFT with proof-of-payment upload
- Email: Brevo for transactional messages and notifications
- Hosting: Vercel
- Other tools: Leaflet/OpenStreetMap for maps, Recharts for analytics, and PWA support


SLIDE 3: FEATURES FOR GIVERS

- Browse verified organizations and open community needs.
- Find needs by category, location, preferences or proximity.
- Offer to help with needs and track interests and deliveries.
- Donate through EFT, PayFast or PayPal, and access receipts.
- Pledge goods, services or funds through the Gift Library.
- Track donations, pledges, messages and fulfillment activity.
- View organization profiles, impact stories and public timelines.


SLIDE 4: FEATURES FOR ORGANIZATIONS

- Create and manage needs, including goods, money or combined support requests.
- Track funding progress and update what is still needed.
- Review giver interests and manage deliveries and fulfillment evidence.
- Receive donations and manage the organization wallet and withdrawal requests.
- Manage team members with owner, manager and coordinator roles.
- Upload verification documents and publish organization stories.
- Share updates, events and milestones on a public timeline.
- Use analytics to view organizational activity and impact.


SLIDE 5: ADMINISTRATOR FEATURES

- Review organizations and verify uploaded documents.
- Moderate needs, Gift Library offerings, claims and impact stories.
- Manage users, organization accounts, roles and platform settings.
- Review withdrawal requests and oversee fulfillment records.
- Send announcements through in-app notifications, email and site banners.
- Monitor live platform activity, login attempts and site-visit reports.
- View analytics and export selected reports.
- Review user inquiries, feedback, developer reports and anonymous tip-offs.


SLIDE 6: AI, ACCESSIBILITY AND SECURITY

AI AND LANGUAGE
- Lifty, a site-wide assistant that can answer HelpLift questions and look up relevant live platform information.
- Voice input, read-aloud replies and optional "Hey Lifty" hands-free chat where supported by the browser and device.
- AI-assisted need creation and photo-based gift-pledge drafting.
- On-demand translation for selected platform content.

ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY
- Speech-to-text, text-to-speech and grammar checking.
- Adjustable text size, reduced motion and optional click sounds.
- Light, dark, high-contrast and grayscale themes.
- Mobile-friendly dashboards and installable PWA with offline awareness.

SECURITY AND TRUST
- Database Row Level Security helps restrict access to data by role.
- Authentication includes email/password, optional two-factor codes, passkeys and supported social sign-in.
- Rate limits and account lockout help protect sensitive flows.
- Organization verification and administrative moderation support platform accountability.


SLIDE 7: CONCLUSION

HelpLift brings community needs, practical giving and transparent follow-through into one platform. By combining donations, in-kind support, organization tools, administrative oversight and accessibility features, it helps people discover ways to contribute and helps organizations manage support from request through delivery.

Note: Security and accessibility features describe implemented safeguards and options; they should not be presented as a certification or guarantee.
